THE RARE LIFE

Set Apart.

The world has a way.
A way of thinking.
A way of living.
A way of defining success, pleasure, identity, truth, and purpose.

Following Jesus means choosing another way.

Following Christ is a call to belong completely to Him, even when obedience separates you from what everyone else considers normal.

“Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind.”

Romans 12:2 ↗

THERE ARE TWO ROADS

Jesus never described following Him as the easiest road.
He described a narrow one.

“The gate is narrow and the way is hard that leads to life, and those who find it are few.”

Matthew 7:14 ↗

Recognize that the direction of the world and the direction of Christ are not the same.

Following Jesus will require you to leave some things behind.

Some habits.
Some desires.
Some relationships.
Some ways of thinking.
Some versions of yourself.

When Christ makes you new, you no longer belong to the life you lived before.

SALVATION

Every person has sinned.

Romans 3:23 ↗

Sin separates us from a holy God.

Romans 6:23 ↗

We cannot repair that separation through good behavior, religion, generosity, morality, church attendance, or becoming a better version of ourselves.

Salvation is found in Jesus Christ.

Jesus lived without sin, died for sinners, and rose from the dead.

John 3:16 ↗1 Corinthians 15:3–4 ↗

We are saved by God’s grace through faith in Christ, not through our own works.

Ephesians 2:8–9 ↗

The call of Christ is a call to repent and believe.

Mark 1:15 ↗

Turn from sin.
Believe the Gospel.
Follow Jesus.

Romans 10:9–10 ↗

MADE NEW

Christianity is not simply adding Jesus to your existing life.
Christianity is the beginning of a new one.

“If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation.”

2 Corinthians 5:17 ↗

A new creation cannot continue living as though nothing has changed.

You will still struggle.
You will still be tempted.
You will still have questions.
You will still need grace.

But following Christ means your direction has changed.

You are learning to love what He loves.
You are learning to reject what separates you from Him.
You are learning to obey when obedience is difficult.

That is part of living a life set apart for Christ.

SET APART

God calls His people to be His.

Not partially.
Not privately.
Not only when convenient.

1 Peter 2:9 ↗1 Peter 1:15–16 ↗

To be set apart means there will be things the world celebrates that you can no longer celebrate.

There will be things everyone else does that you may need to stop doing.
There will be desires you will have to deny.
There will be moments when obedience costs you something.

Following Christ has a cost.
Jesus told us to count it.

Luke 14:27–33 ↗

LESS OF THE WORLD

A life set apart for Christ is often defined as much by what you leave behind as by what you begin doing.

The world tells you to satisfy yourself.
Jesus says deny yourself.

Luke 9:23 ↗

The world tells you to follow your heart.
Scripture tells you to trust the Lord.

Proverbs 3:5–7 ↗

The world tells you to fit in.
Christ calls you to be transformed.

Romans 12:1–2 ↗

The world tells you that your life belongs to you.
Christ calls you to follow Him.

Matthew 16:24–25 ↗

The goal is not isolation from people.
You need community.

The goal is separation from the patterns that pull you away from God.

SALT AND LIGHT

Being set apart means showing up differently in the world.

Matthew 5:13–16 ↗

You are called to live differently where people can see it.

At home.
At work.
Among friends.
In relationships.
In private.
When nobody is watching.

A life changed by Christ should eventually become visible.

People shouldn’t admire you.
They should see Him.

WHERE DO I START?

Start with Jesus.

Not Christian culture.
Not arguments on the internet.
Not trying to learn everything at once.

Get to know Christ.

Open the Bible.
Read His words.
Ask questions.
Think carefully.
Pray for wisdom.
Find faithful biblical teaching.

Then keep going.

“Like newborn infants, long for the pure spiritual milk, that by it you may grow up into salvation.”

1 Peter 2:2 ↗

If you are new to Scripture, begin with the Gospel of John.

Read slowly.
Do not rush to finish chapters.
Pay attention to who Jesus says He is.
Pay attention to what He says.
Pay attention to what people do when they encounter Him.

Then read it again.

BECOME HUNGRY

Do not settle for knowing about Jesus.
Know Him.

Do not settle for hearing Scripture once a week.
Study it.

Do not settle for a Christianity that changes nothing.
Follow Him.

John 8:31–32 ↗James 1:22 ↗Psalm 119:105 ↗

The Christian life is not a moment.
It is a life of following Christ.

Learning.
Repenting.
Growing.
Obeying.
Enduring.

LIVE SET APART

Living set apart is not perfection.

But…

It is a life of surrender.
It is choosing Christ when another path would be easier.
It is refusing to let culture determine what is good, true, or worthy.
It is becoming less attached to the world and more devoted to the One who saved you.

The road is narrow.
The cost is real.
Christ is worth it.

Follow Him.
