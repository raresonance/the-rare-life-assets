LESS OF THE WORLD

A life set apart for Christ is often defined as much by what you leave behind as by what you begin doing.

The world tells you to satisfy yourself.
Jesus says deny yourself.

Luke 9:23 ↗

The world tells you to follow your heart.
Scripture tells you to trust the Lord.

Proverbs 3:5–7 ↗

The world tells you to fit in.
Christ calls you to be transformed.

Romans 12:1–2 ↗

The world tells you that your life belongs to you.
Christ calls you to follow Him.

Matthew 16:24–25 ↗

The goal is not isolation from people.
You need community.

The goal is separation from the patterns that pull you away from God.

SALT AND LIGHT

Being set apart means showing up differently in the world.

Matthew 5:13–16 ↗

You are called to live differently where people can see it.

At home.
At work.
Among friends.
In relationships.
In private.
When nobody is watching.

A life changed by Christ should eventually become visible.

People shouldn’t admire you.
They should see Him.
